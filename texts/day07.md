# Day 7 - Laboratories

> https://adventofcode.com/2025/day/7

## What We Need To Do

A tachyon beam enters a manifold at `S` and moves downward. When it hits a splitter (`^`), the beam stops and two new beams continue from the left and right of the splitter.

Example:
```
.......S.......
...............
.......^.......
...............
......^.^......
...............
.....^.^.^.....
...............
....^.^...^....
...............
...^.^...^.^...
...............
..^...^.....^..
...............
.^.^.^.^.^...^.
...............
```

### Part One

Count how many times the beam is split (i.e., how many splitters are actually hit by a beam coming from above).

The beam enters at `S` and travels down. When it hits a `^`, it splits left and right:

```
.......S.......       .......S.......       .......S.......
.......|.......       .......|.......       .......|.......
.......^.......   →   ......|^|......   →   ......|^|......
...............       ...............       ......|.|......
......^.^......       ......^.^......       ......^.^......
```

The process continues until all beams exit or hit splitters:

```
.......S.......
.......|.......
......|^|......
......|.|......
.....|^|^|.....
.....|.|.|.....
....|^|^|^|....
....|.|.|.|....
...|^|^|||^|...
...|.|.|||.|...
..|^|^|||^|^|..
..|.|.|||.|.|..
.|^|||^||.||^|.
.|.|||.||.||.|.
|^|^|^|^|^|||^|
|.|.|.|.|.|||.|
```

A total of **21** splitters are hit.
